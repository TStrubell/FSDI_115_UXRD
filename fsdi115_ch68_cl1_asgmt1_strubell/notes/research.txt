ASSIGNMENT 1: UX COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS & USER RESEARCH 

PROJECT NAME: Strubell Goods & Gear (SGnG)

TEAM MEMBER: Tim Strubell

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
  Strubell Goods & Gear is a premium handmade leather goods brand focused on providing customers with high-quality wallets, belts, keychains, and travel accessories through a modern e-commerce experience.

=========================================================
SECTION 1 - PROJECT INFORMATION
=========================================================

PROJECT GOAL:
  The objective of SGnG is to create an intuitive, visually appealing, and responsive e-commerce platform that highlights craftsmanship while providing a frictionless shopping experience.

TARGET AUDIENCE:

- Professionals seeking premium accessories
- Travelers
- Outdoor enthusiasts
- Customers interested in handcrafted products
- Gift shoppers

Primary User Goals

1. Browse products easily.
2. Learn about product craftsmanship.
3. Compare product options.
4. Purchase products quickly.
5. Complete checkout with minimal effort.

=========================================================
SECTION 2 - COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS
=========================================================

COMPETITOR #1: BELLROY

What Bellroy Does Well:

  - Intuitive navigation
  - Minimalist user interface
  - High-quality photography
  - Responsive mobile experience
  - Fast browsing experience

Usability Issues

  - Product details occasionally require excessive scrolling.
  - Some informational content may overwhelm new users.

Ideas to Incorporate into SGnG

  - Clean product cards
  - Larger images
  - Simplified navigation
  - Sticky navigation bar
  - Streamlined checkout flow

---------------------------------------------------------

COMPETITOR #2: SADDLEBACK LEATHER

What Saddleback Leather Does Well

  - Strong storytelling
  - Detailed product descriptions
  - Effective trust-building
  - Showcases craftsmanship

Usability Issues

  - Content-heavy pages
  - Navigation could be simplified
  - Product discovery can require extra effort

Ideas to Incorporate into SGnG

  - Highlight craftsmanship
  - Present material details clearly
  - Showcase durability
  - Create engaging brand stories

---------------------------------------------------------

COMPETITOR #3: HUCKBERRY

What Huckberry Does Well

  - Modern website design
  - Excellent product discovery
  - Consistent branding
  - Effective product recommendations

Usability Issues

  - Large catalog may create decision fatigue.
  - Promotional content can distract from purchasing flow.

Ideas to Incorporate into SGnG

  - Product recommendations
  - Curated collections
  - Improved filtering options
  - Responsive catalog layout

=========================================================
SECTION 3 - REFLECTION
=========================================================

Which Application Provides the Best UX?

  Bellroy provides the best overall user experience because of its clean visual design, intuitive navigation, and strong mobile responsiveness.

Common UX Patterns Observed

  - Large product images
  - Clear product descriptions
  - Consistent branding
  - Simple navigation systems
  - Mobile-first layouts
  - Streamlined shopping experiences

Improvements That Will Make the Biggest Difference

  1. Responsive design implementation.
  2. Better product presentation.
  3. Simplified checkout process.
  4. Improved navigation structure.
  5. Enhanced mobile usability.

Top Three Features Planned for SGnG

  1. Responsive product catalog.
  2. Detailed product pages.
  3. Streamlined cart and checkout functionality.

=========================================================
CONCLUSION
=========================================================
  The competitive analysis demonstrates that successful e-commerce experiences prioritize usability, responsiveness, visual clarity, and customer trust. SGnG will combine the strongest elements from Bellroy, Saddleback Leather, and Huckberry to create a premium shopping experience that reflects the brand’s craftsmanship and quality standards.
