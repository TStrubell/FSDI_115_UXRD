SGnG USABILITY TEST — DOCUMENTATION
FSDI 115 | Tim Strubell | Assignment 2 | Fall 2026
=====================================================

TEST OVERVIEW
-------------
Method:       Moderated Remote Usability Test
Platform:     Zoom (screen share + think-aloud protocol)
Prototype:    Figma clickable prototype — 5 screens, linked flows
Date:         Week 4, Fall 2026
Participants: 5 (recruited from personal network; none had seen SGnG before)

PARTICIPANT PROFILES
--------------------
P1: Male, 34, marketing manager, frequent online shopper, uses iPhone + MacBook
P2: Female, 28, graphic designer, buys premium goods online monthly
P3: Male, 52, small business owner, moderate tech comfort, Windows user
P4: Female, 41, teacher, shops online 1-2x/month, uses iPad primarily
P5: Male, 26, graduate student, daily online shopper, high tech comfort

TASKS ASSIGNED
--------------
Task 1: "You want to buy the Classic Leather Wallet. Find it and add it to your cart."
Task 2: "Complete the checkout and find your order confirmation."

TEST RESULTS
------------

                    P1    P2    P3    P4    P5    Rate
Task 1 Completed?   YES   YES   YES   YES   YES   100%
Task 2 Completed?   YES   YES   NO*   YES   YES    80%
Time on Task 1      3m    2m    5m    4m    2m    3.2m avg
Time on Task 2      4m    3m    7m    5m    3m    4.4m avg

*P3 could not locate "Proceed to Checkout" button without assistance (low contrast on his screen)

WHAT WAS EASY
-------------
- P1: "The navigation is super clean. I found the wallet in about 10 seconds."
- P2: "The amber buttons are obvious — I always knew exactly where to click."
- P4: "Product photos are really clear. I could see the leather texture easily."
- P5: "Everything makes sense. The flow feels natural — browse, details, cart, done."
- All 5: Identified the cart icon immediately. Nav was universally praised.

WHAT WAS CONFUSING
------------------
- P3: "The checkout button blended into the dark background on my monitor."
- P4: "I didn't know if I could change the quantity in the cart — there was no +/- control."
- P1: "The confirmation page didn't show me WHEN my order would arrive — I wanted a date."
- P2: "I wasn't sure if 'Our Story' was about the brand or about my account history."
- P5: "The mobile hamburger menu didn't feel tappy enough — icon was a bit small."

IMPROVEMENTS MADE
-----------------
1. CART — Added quantity selector (+ / - stepper) to Cart screen
   Reason: 3/5 participants expected to change qty directly in cart
   
2. PRODUCT DETAIL — Increased "Add to Cart" button height from 44px to 52px
   Reason: Tap target too small on mobile view (WCAG 2.5.5 target size)
   
3. CONFIRMATION — Added estimated delivery window (e.g., "Est. delivery: Sep 21–24")
   Reason: All 5 participants expected a delivery estimate on the confirmation screen
   
4. CATALOG — Added "aria-current=page" to active nav link for screen reader support
   Reason: Accessibility audit flagged missing active state for keyboard/screen reader users

SUS SCORE (System Usability Scale)
-----------------------------------
10-question SUS survey sent post-test via Google Form

Scores:
  P1: 85    P2: 90    P3: 70    P4: 80    P5: 85
  Average: 82 / 100
  Grade: B — "Good" (above the industry average of 68)

CONCLUSION
----------
The SGnG Figma prototype performed well above average on the SUS scale (82 vs. 68 industry avg).
The amber CTA system was universally praised as clear and intuitive. Primary improvements
targeted quantity editing in the cart, button tap targets, and delivery estimate visibility
on the confirmation screen. P3's low contrast issue prompted a review of button contrast
ratios to ensure WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 minimum) at all viewport sizes.
